# Privacy Policy — Global Data Centre Map

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

Global Data Centre Map ("the app", package `com.happylab.datacentremap`) is published by Happylab_26.

## Summary
The app does **not** collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

## What the app accesses
- **Data centre information:** When opened, the app downloads a public data file from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`). As with any website request, GitHub may receive your device's IP address as part of normal internet traffic. See GitHub's Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
- **Maps:** The map is provided by Google Maps Platform. Google may collect information as described in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Local storage:** The app saves the most recently downloaded data file on your device so it can work offline. This contains no personal information and is deleted when you uninstall the app.
- The app does **not** request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Data sources
Data centre information is compiled from public sources, including Epoch AI "AI data centers" (CC BY 4.0). Information may be incomplete or out of date and is provided for general information only.

## Changes
Updates to this policy will be posted at this page.

## Contact
Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/na3hara-debug/datacentremap-data/issues
